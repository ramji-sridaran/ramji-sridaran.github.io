# Family Tree Design Instructions

## Overall Goal

Create a visually clean, intuitive, and professional family tree that is easy to navigate, even for large families. Prioritize readability, consistency, and logical organization over fitting everything into the smallest possible space.

## Layout Rules

* Keep spouses/couples together at all times. Their tiles should always appear side by side with a clear visual connection.
* Never separate a couple across different rows or sections unless absolutely unavoidable.
* Children should always appear directly below their parents.
* Maintain consistent generation levels. Everyone in the same generation should be aligned horizontally whenever possible.
* Avoid crossing relationship lines. If multiple layout options exist, choose the one with the fewest crossing lines.
* Keep adequate spacing between families so the tree never feels cluttered.
* Use a balanced layout that minimizes excessive horizontal or vertical stretching.

## Family Grouping

* Assign a unique color theme to each immediate family (parents and their children).
* All members belonging to the same nuclear family should share the same primary color.
* Preserve the same color across the entire tree wherever that family appears.
* Use color only as a visual aid; do not rely on color alone to communicate relationships.

## Color Guidelines

* Use soft, muted, professional colors.
* Avoid harsh, highly saturated, fluorescent, or neon colors.
* Prefer pastel or low-saturation tones.
* Ensure sufficient contrast between text and background for readability.
* Keep colors visually distinct while remaining harmonious.

Recommended palette examples:

* Soft Blue
* Sage Green
* Dusty Purple
* Warm Beige
* Muted Teal
* Peach
* Lavender
* Light Olive
* Soft Coral
* Slate Blue

## Visual Consistency

* All person tiles should have identical dimensions.
* Use consistent spacing between tiles.
* Maintain consistent font sizes throughout the tree.
* Use one font family for the entire diagram.
* Use subtle rounded corners.
* Keep connector lines uniform in thickness and style.
* Avoid unnecessary shadows, gradients, or decorative effects.

## Information Display

Each person tile should consistently display:

* Full name
* Birth year (if available)
* Death year (if applicable)
* Optional profile photo
* Gender indicator (optional)
* Important notes only when requested

Avoid overcrowding the tiles.

## Relationship Display

* Marriage connections should be visually distinct from parent-child connections.
* Clearly indicate multiple marriages while keeping all spouses aligned logically.
* Adopted children should be shown using a clearly distinguishable connector style.
* Step relationships should also have a distinct visual style.
* If relationship status is unknown, indicate it subtly without disrupting the layout.

## Readability

* Optimize the tree for both desktop and mobile viewing.
* Ensure text remains legible when zoomed out.
* Minimize long connector lines.
* Prevent overlapping nodes or text.
* Keep the overall structure visually balanced.

## Navigation (for interactive trees)

* Support smooth zooming and panning.
* Allow collapsing and expanding family branches.
* Highlight the selected person's immediate relatives.
* Preserve layout positions when expanding or collapsing branches.

## Accessibility

* Use color combinations that are distinguishable for users with common forms of color vision deficiency.
* Maintain sufficient text contrast.
* Do not rely solely on color to indicate relationships.

## Aesthetic Principles

* Favor simplicity over decoration.
* Keep the design clean and modern.
* Use generous white space.
* Maintain symmetry wherever practical.
* Ensure the tree looks organized regardless of family size.

## Stability Rules

* Avoid rearranging existing branches unnecessarily when new members are added.
* Preserve positions of existing families whenever possible.
* Insert new members with minimal disruption to the current layout.

## Quality Checklist

Before finalizing the family tree, verify that:

* Every couple remains together.
* Every child is correctly connected to their parent(s).
* No relationship lines cross unnecessarily.
* Family colors are applied consistently.
* Text is readable.
* Spacing is uniform.
* Colors are soft and visually balanced.
* The tree remains easy to understand even for someone seeing it for the first time.
